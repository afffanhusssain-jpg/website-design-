Questions

Can I put this on my car?

Yes, on the front, in Georgia and other states that only require a rear plate. It is a decorative plate, so it never takes the place of your state-issued one. If your state requires a front registration plate, keep this one for the wall or the show field.

What sizes do you make?

US standard is 12 × 6 inches and fits regular US brackets. Euro style is the long 20.5 × 4.3 inch shape. Motorcycle is 7 × 4 inches.

Can I get a color that is not in the presets?

Yes. Under Colors, set the plate, the lettering and the border to any color you like, and turn on the two-tone fade if you want the plate to blend between two colors.

Is there anything you will not make?

We do not make plates that copy an official state plate, carry government or law-enforcement markings, or use a brand's logo without permission.
